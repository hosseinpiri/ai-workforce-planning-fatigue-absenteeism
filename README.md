# When Workforce Well-Being Shapes Project Performance

Saeed Aramesh, Babak Abbasi, Ron Wakefield (RMIT University) and Hossein Piri (Haskayne School of Business, University of Calgary).

Working paper, *Production and Operations Management*.

- [Download the PDF](Workforce_WellBeing_Project_Performance.pdf)

## Abstract

Residential construction projects are delivered through labor-intensive tasks under tight deadlines, yet current project scheduling and staff planning models usually treat workforce capacity as fixed and overlook human-centric factors such as labor fatigue, fairness in fatigue exposure, fatigue-induced quality degradation, and fatigue-related absenteeism. This paper introduces an integrated personnel staffing and project scheduling model for residential construction that progresses to a human-centric-aware planning framework. A fatigue activation mechanism reveals the operational impact of assigned workload on workforce fatigue, and the human-centric factors are incorporated sequentially to isolate how each reshapes staffing and scheduling decisions.
